One correction before anything else

The rate in your Tally is 5%, not 18%

You said 18% — 9 + 9 split, or 18 as IGST. Every voucher in the recording says otherwise. On a taxable value of ₹13,47,625.00 of SOYA BEAN MEAL:

CGST ₹33,690.63 + SGST ₹33,690.63 — that is 2.5% + 2.5%. And on the inter-state voucher to BETUL OIL LTD, IGST ₹67,381.25 — 5%.

5% is also the correct statutory rate for oil-cake and solvent-extracted meal under HSN 2304, and it is what your existing Soya module already uses (CGST 2.5 / SGST 2.5). I have planned for 5%, stored per item as an HSN-linked rate rather than hard-coded — so if some other item genuinely is 18%, it is a field, not a code change. Tell me if 18% was meant for a specific product and I will set that item's rate.

Your sales ledger stands at ₹14,27,66,780 — well past the ₹5 crore turnover threshold, so e-invoicing is mandatory, not optional. The recording shows you already working the NIC portal directly, including the pin-to-pin distance lookup (413531 → 577217 = 587 km) for the e-way bill.

07

Build order

Each phase ends somewhere usable. The live maize side is never touched — this is new tables and new routes throughout, the same discipline the Soya line was built with.

  1. Company as the top-level switch

    A company master carrying legal name, GSTIN, state code, address, pincode and invoice prefix. Sidebar re-roots on the open company. The two existing firms load straight in.

    Ships: switch companies, see an empty Factory and Parties beneath each

  2. Masters with statutory fields

    Factory and Party masters gain GSTIN, registration type, state, pincode and address, with GSTIN format validation and state auto-derived from its first two digits. Item master with HSN, unit of measure and GST rate.

    Ships: the data entry that everything downstream depends on

  3. Tax invoice

    Invoice entry with place of supply, line items, and CGST/SGST/IGST derived — never typed. Freight and round-off as separate lines, matching the Tally voucher. Printable tax invoice in the format you already use.

    Ships: a legally-formatted tax invoice you can hand over

  4. e-Invoice round trip

    INV-01 JSON export, response import, IRN and signed QR stored against the invoice and printed on it. A queue screen showing which invoices are pending, registered or failed.

    Ships: portal compliance without re-keying

  5. Credit notes & e-Way Bill

    Credit notes against an original invoice, carrying the same tax treatment. E-way bill fields — transporter, distance, vehicle, Part B — on invoices that need one.

    Ships: the remaining document types from the recording

  6. GST returns view

    B2B and B2C summaries by period, reconciling to GSTR-1 so your accountant can tie out the month without a manual extract.

    Ships: the month-end that this whole structure exists to serve
